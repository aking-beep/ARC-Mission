# ARC Mission

**By ARC Transformation Group**

> **Technology for a greater purpose.**
>
> Helping mission-driven organizations become more sustainable, resilient, visible and
> capable of serving humanity.

ARC Mission brings enterprise-grade digital transformation, artificial intelligence and
applied research to organizations solving humanitarian and social problems. It is meant
to be a signature practice of ARC Transformation Group — the social-impact flagship that
shows what happens when advanced technology is applied to human problems rather than
exclusively commercial ones — and not merely another product in the portfolio.

This document is the brief. It records what ARC Mission is intended to become. What Labs
is actually testing, and what would stop it, is in the record:
[`exp-2026-0007-arc-mission`](https://github.com/aking-beep/arc-labs/blob/HEAD/incubator/validating/exp-2026-0007-arc-mission.md).

**Status as of 2026-11-21:** experiment. A working prototype of all five modules exists
in [this repository](../README.md)
— open `index.html`. No nonprofit has used it yet, and the name has not been cleared.

## The platform

A digital transformation and sustainability platform for nonprofits. Five capabilities,
delivered as modules of **one** platform rather than five separate products.

| # | Module | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Mission Intelligence** | Understand organizational health, financial vulnerabilities, operational inefficiencies and growth opportunities. |
| 02 | **Mission Simulator** | Model how investments, funding changes and operational decisions could affect sustainability and service capacity. |
| 03 | **Mission Funding** | Identify relevant grants, sponsors, donations, technology credits and professional resources. |
| 04 | **Mission Reach** | Improve digital awareness, accessibility, storytelling and supporter engagement. |
| 05 | **Mission Outcomes** | Track improvements, measure program delivery and generate evidence-backed impact reports. |

All five exist in the prototype. Mission Funding goes furthest: it checks eligibility
against 16 real technology programs, links into real grant databases and drafts
application answers from the organization's own figures. A Transformation section turns
the practice below into an assessment, an admin-work inventory and a 90-day roadmap. Mission Intelligence is the one the
evidence is about, because the other four consume what it produces.

## The transformation practice

The software is one part of the opportunity. ARC Transformation Group provides the
expertise to help nonprofits implement the changes the platform identifies, which gives
the product and the consulting capability a natural relationship: the platform finds the
work, the practice does it, and the outcomes flow back into the platform.

| Service | What ARC delivers |
| --- | --- |
| Digital strategy | Technology assessment and transformation roadmap |
| Digital experience | Websites, donation journeys and accessibility |
| AI transformation | Responsible AI implementation |
| Process automation | Reduced administrative workloads |
| Data transformation | Analytics, dashboards and data infrastructure |
| Financial intelligence | Sustainability modeling and scenario planning |
| Digital awareness | Discoverability and supporter engagement |
| Technology governance | Security, privacy and responsible technology adoption |

The practice is **not** Labs work. Client delivery has obligations an experiment does not,
so it sits with ARC Transformation Group and is recorded in the `boundary:` section of
[`portfolio/products.yaml`](https://github.com/aking-beep/arc-labs/blob/HEAD/portfolio/products.yaml). What Labs takes from it is
evidence: every engagement is a chance to test a module against a real organization.

## The ARC Mission Transformation Program

The flagship initiative, and how ARC builds its reputation in the sector. Every quarter,
ARC selects a nonprofit and helps it solve one tangible digital or operational challenge.

| Quarter | Transformation project | Module it exercises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Modernize a nonprofit's website and fundraising experience | Mission Reach |
| Q2 | Automate administrative workflows for a grassroots organization | Mission Intelligence |
| Q3 | Build a data and impact measurement platform | Mission Outcomes |
| Q4 | Implement predictive financial and operational intelligence | Mission Simulator |

Each project produces a published case study — **with the nonprofit's consent** —
showing what ARC delivered and what improved. A case study with no measured change is
still published, as a finding.

## Naming

**ARC Mission** is the working choice. It reads as human, states the purpose, and sits
naturally beside ARC Control and ARC Labs — which is why it was preferred over the earlier
working name, ARC Leverage.

| Name | Brand positioning |
| --- | --- |
| **ARC Mission** | Technology for mission-driven organizations |
| ARC Catalyst | Accelerating organizational transformation |
| ARC Horizon | Forward-looking sustainability intelligence |
| ARC Enable | Empowering organizations through technology |
| ARC Elevate | Expanding organizational capacity |
| ARC Humanity | Technology for human progress |
| ARC Basis | Strengthening nonprofit foundations |

**Not yet done:** formal trademark and domain checks. They are required before any public
launch. Some attractive alternatives are already taken — ARC Impact has businesses
operating under similar names — so the working name should be treated as provisional
until it is cleared.

## What has to be true before this is real

1. A first nonprofit is named and has agreed to take part.
2. That nonprofit's leader has used the prototype end to end
   ([Gate 2](https://github.com/aking-beep/arc-labs/blob/HEAD/docs/GRADUATION_CRITERIA.md#gate-2--experiment--prototype)).
3. The name is cleared.
4. There is a written position on how a nonprofit's financial and beneficiary data is
   handled — none of it is ever stored in this repository.
